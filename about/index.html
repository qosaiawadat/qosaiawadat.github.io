The long version.

Qosai Awadat is a finance professional and entrepreneur who lives between Texas and Bahrain. Most recently an associate on the finance team at Infracorp in Manama, he was a budget analyst at At Home Group in Dallas before that, and graduated from Queen Mary University of London with a BSc in Economics and Finance. The rest of the time he builds things: TriviaDerby, a bilingual trivia game for the majlis, and Design by ATHAR, a web design studio.
